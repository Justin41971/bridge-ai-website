01 / THE PROBLEM

Educational opportunity should not depend on income.

Private tutoring can be expensive, school support can be limited, and students who need help may not know where to find someone who can teach them.

Bridge focuses on connecting students who need academic help with students who have the knowledge and time to help.

04 / BRIDGE AI

AI works in the background throughout the lesson.

Bridge AI is a teaching assistant. The tutor remains responsible for teaching the student.

BEFORE THE SESSION

Turn a student's problem into a teachable lesson.

  • Analyze the student's microtopic
  • Break the concept into teachable steps
  • Create examples and practice questions
  • Adapt material to the student's level

  • Translate learning material
  • Adapt material to the student's understanding
