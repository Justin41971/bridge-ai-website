Bridge treats tutoring as a matching problem. The platform evaluates both sides of the connection before deciding which student should receive which tutor.

07 / GLOBAL ACCESS

Bridge can go beyond the classroom.

The idea behind Bridge is larger than connecting two people online. It is about connecting educational opportunity with communities that have historically had less access to it.

GLOBAL ACCESS MODEL AFRICA

THE PROBLEM Unreliable connectivity

Students may have internet access only at school, libraries or community locations. Bridge's offline system is designed around that constraint.

THE APPROACH Download once. Learn repeatedly.

A student can download a complete learning package and continue working without needing continuous internet access.

THE BRIDGE Human tutors + AI + offline access

Bridge combines human instruction with AI assistance and offline resources rather than assuming every learner has the same technology.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Technology should adapt to the learner's environment — not force the learner to adapt to the technology.
