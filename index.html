THE PROBLEM

Students do not have equal access to education.

Students from low-income communities may not have access to private tutors, academic mentors, paid worksheets or reliable technology.

Even students who want to help others often have no simple way to find people who need help.

Scheduling can also be difficult because every student has different availability.
