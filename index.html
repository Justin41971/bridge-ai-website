01 / THE PROBLEM

Educational opportunity should not depend on income.

Private tutoring can be expensive, school support can be limited, and finding someone who can help at the right time is difficult.

Bridge is designed for students who have fewer educational opportunities and students who have the knowledge and time to help.

77–87% Project research states that this percentage of low-income students in Ontario do not have access to private tutoring.
