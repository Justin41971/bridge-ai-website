THE PROBLEM

Education should not depend on what you can afford.

Students from lower-income communities may not have access to private tutoring, paid worksheets, academic mentors or reliable technology.

At the same time, many students already have the knowledge and willingness to help someone else — but have no easy way to find them.

Bridge connects these two groups and organizes the process around the student's actual educational need.

01 Private tutoring can be expensive.
02 Schedules make tutoring difficult to organize.
03 Educational resources can be inaccessible.
04 Students who can help often do not know where to start.
