01 / THE PROBLEM

Educational opportunity should not depend on income.

Private tutoring can be expensive, school support can be limited, and finding someone who can help at the right time is difficult.

Bridge is designed for students who have fewer educational opportunities and for students who have the knowledge and time to help.

77–87% Project research estimate for low-income students without access to private tutoring in Ontario.
11% Project research figure for Canadian households with limited access to reliable Wi-Fi.
40% / 60%+ The project highlights larger connectivity barriers in some major cities and Indigenous communities.

07 / OFFLINE ACCESS

Learning can continue when Wi-Fi cannot.

Students who only have reliable internet at school, a library or another access point can download a package and use it later.

Bridge is not fixing the internet problem. It is reducing the educational resource barrier created by it.
