01 / THE PROBLEM

Educational opportunity should not depend on income.

Private tutoring can be expensive, school support can be limited, and finding someone who can help at the right time is difficult.

At the same time, there are students who already have the knowledge and time to help but have no simple way to connect with students who need it.

77–87% of low-income students described in the project research as lacking access to private tutoring in Ontario.
11% of Canadian households described in the project as having limited reliable Wi-Fi access.
60%+ connectivity barriers highlighted for some Indigenous communities in the project research.

06 / OFFLINE LEARNING

Learning continues when Wi-Fi cannot.

A student can download a complete learning package while they have access to Wi-Fi and use it later without a live connection.

Live video is not required. Students can also communicate through messages, explanations, images and practice questions.
