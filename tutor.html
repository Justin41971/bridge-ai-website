02 / AI TEACHING ASSESSMENT

What happens when AI becomes your student?

Every applicant receives a standardized teaching scenario. Bridge AI acts as the student while evaluating how the tutor explains and adapts.

01
Standardized prompt

Every tutor can receive the same teaching scenario so applications can be compared consistently.

02
AI becomes the learner

The AI can behave as confused, inexperienced, language-limited or otherwise require adaptation.

03
Bridge evaluates

The system can assess accuracy, clarity, pacing, adaptation, reasoning and respectful teaching.

04
Human review

For a real implementation, AI results could be combined with supervision or human review.

03 / TUTOR QUALITY

Don't just measure what tutors know.

A strong tutor needs more than a high grade. Bridge can evaluate whether someone can actually communicate knowledge to another person.

01 Knowledge accuracy

Does the tutor explain the concept correctly?

02 Clarity

Can the tutor explain complicated ideas simply?

03 Adaptation

Can the tutor change their explanation when the student doesn't understand?

04 Pacing

Does the tutor give the learner enough time to process new information?

05 Communication

Is the tutor respectful, understandable and encouraging?

06 Reflection

Can the tutor identify what went well and what they should improve?
