Teaching quality should be measurable.

Bridge can use an AI teaching simulation as one component of tutor evaluation. It is designed to test how applicants explain, adapt and respond to misunderstandings.

01

Can they make a difficult concept simple?

  • Clarity
  • Correctness
  • Appropriate examples
02
ADAPT

Can they change their explanation?

  • Detects confusion
  • Uses alternative explanations
  • Adjusts difficulty
03
TEACH

Can they guide instead of giving answers?

  • Socratic questioning
  • Encourages reasoning
  • Gives useful feedback
